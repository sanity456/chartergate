# CharterGate demo — about 90 seconds

Record only the application tab. Keep wallet popups, notifications, passwords and unrelated tabs out of the recording. This script is a guide, not a completed video.

## 0–12 seconds — purpose

Show the home screen and Studio Next label.

“CharterGate helps a community check proposals against its charter before voting. The screening and voting gate live in a GenLayer intelligent contract.”

## 12–25 seconds — rules

Select **CharterGate Wallet Test**, then **Community charter**. Show its three rules, one-wallet voter list and quorum of one. Return to Proposals.

“This test community requires free participation, open-source development teaching and openly published learning materials. These rules, membership and quorum are fixed when the community is created.”

## 25–40 seconds — eligible and passed

Open **Free open-source developer workshop**. Scroll to rule evidence and its closed PASSED ballot.

“The first proposal passed all three rules. Here are the cited statements and reasons. The approved test vote and ballot closure were recorded on-chain: one Yes, zero No.”

## 40–54 seconds — rejection

Go to All proposals; open **Paid open-source developer workshop**.

“This proposal charges an attendance fee. That contradicts the charter, so it is ineligible. Voting is blocked by the contract, not just hidden in the interface.”

## 54–75 seconds — clarification and immutable revision

Open **Open-source developer meetup**, then return and open **Open-source developer meetup (clarified)**. Click **View preserved original**.

“Missing information is a separate result: needs clarification. Adding an explicit free-attendance statement created an eligible revision. The new record links to the original, which keeps its unchanged decision and evidence.”

## 75–90 seconds — proof and limitations

Show footer links to the contract and wallet test evidence, then finish on the app overview.

“The source and test evidence are available for review. This is a low-stakes Studio Next prototype, not a treasury or production DAO. AI screening can misinterpret text, so always inspect the evidence.”
